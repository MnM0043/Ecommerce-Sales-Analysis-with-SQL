## What this business does

The business is a multi-category ecommerce retailer selling products across four main categories: **Electronics, Apparel, Beauty, and Home**. Its catalog covers a broad range of products, from headphones and smartwatches to clothing, skincare, kitchen products, and home furnishings.

The available data covers customer purchases from **16 March to 14 June 2026**, providing roughly three months of business activity to explore.

## Scale

The dataset contains **40,000 orders from 8,800 unique customers**, equivalent to an average of **4.55 orders per customer** during the available period.

The retailer carries **4,000 products**, of which **3,867 (96.7%) are currently active**. The active assortment is fairly distributed across the four main categories:

- **Electronics:** 1,106 active products
- **Apparel:** 1,089 active products
- **Beauty:** 867 active products
- **Home:** 805 active products

The data connects orders to individual customers and products, providing a foundation for deeper analysis of customer purchasing behaviour and product performance.

## What I noticed

The most striking initial finding is the difference between the retailer's **product mix and revenue mix**.

**Electronics represents only 28.6% of the active product catalog but generates 57.17% of paid revenue.** Apparel contributes another **21.46%**, meaning Electronics and Apparel together account for **78.63% of paid revenue**.

This suggests that while the retailer maintains a relatively broad product assortment, its commercial performance is considerably more concentrated. **Electronics appears disproportionately important to the business relative to the size of its catalog.**

The dataset covers only about three months, with March and June being partial months. Longer-term growth and seasonal patterns therefore cannot yet be established reliably.
